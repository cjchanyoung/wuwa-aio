Substat Tuning Priority Guide

Tuning Guidelines

Echos must be leveled to multiples of 5 (5, 10, 15, 20, 25) to unlock Substat slots. Tuning requires Premium Tuners relative to the Echo rarity.

Once a substat is tuned, it cannot be rerolled. If you roll low-value stats (e.g. DEF, HP on a main carry), it is usually optimal to feed the Echo into another raw base Echo for 80% exp return.

Substat Values (5★ Echos)

  • Crit Rate: 6.3% ~ 10.5%
  • Crit DMG: 12.6% ~ 21.0%
  • Resonance Skill/Lib DMG: 6.4% ~ 12.4%
  • Energy Regeneration: 7.4% ~ 12.4%
