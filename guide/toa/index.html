Tower of Adversity Guide

Hazard Zone floor mechanics, active leyline buffs, and high-performance speedrun team formations.

Vigor Mechanics

Each Resonator has limited vigor points (max 10). Use backup characters on lower floors and reserve your maxed-out hypercarries for the 4th floor nuke nodes.

30-Star Clear Target

Clear within the target speed thresholds (usually 300+ seconds remaining) to claim full Astrites and Hazard Record tokens from the shop.

Active Leyline Buffs

Every version update refreshes the element specific buffs (e.g. +30% Spectro and Havoc DMG). Align your custom builds to current floor modifiers.
