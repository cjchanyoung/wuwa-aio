Electro Rectifier Sub DPS / Amp

Yinlin

A former patroller of the Jinzhou Security Department, working under the cover of secrecy to hunt down the Fractsidus. Yinlin serves as an exceptional off-field Sub-DPS who inflicts coordinated Electro damage and amplifies team Ultimate and Electro damage output.

(5★ Resonator)

Weapon Recommendations

Stringmaster 5★ Signature

Base ATK: 500 • Crit Rate: 36.0% (Lvl 90)

Increases Crit Rate and boosts Resonance Attribute DMG by 12%. When casting Resonance Skill or when dealing damage off-field, ATK increases by 12% for 2 stacks. The absolute best choice for her dual on-field/off-field role.

Rectifier #25 4★ Best Alternative

Base ATK: 412 • ATK%: 30.3% (Lvl 90)

When Resonance Skill is cast, increases ATK by 12% for 15s. Generates extra energy to help maintain critical ultimate uptime. Very accessible F2P option.
