Gameplay Guidelines & Tips

Yinlin is an exceptional off-field Sub-DPS who provides massive Electro DMG amplification and coordinated attacks. Trigger her Forte Gauge completely to apply Sinner's Mark, and swap-cancel her heavy attacks to maximize team rotation speed.
