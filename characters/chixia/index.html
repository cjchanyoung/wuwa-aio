Recommended Sequences

S1: No.1 Hero Play Fan Recommended

Chixia's Resonance Skill ("Boom Boom" hits) will always be a Critical Hit.

S2: Leaping Sparkles

During Resonance Liberation, for every target defeated, Chixia recovers 5 Resonance Energy (up to 20 per Liberation).

S3: Eternal Flames

Resonance Liberation deals 40% more damage to targets with HP below 50%.

S4: Hero's Ultimate Move Recommended

Resonance Liberation grants 60 "Thermobaric Bullets" and immediately resets the cooldown of her Resonance Skill.

S5: Triumphant Explosions

When inherent skill ("Numbingly Spicy!") reaches max stacks, her ATK is increased by 30%.

S6: Easter Egg Performance

Resonance Skill increases the Basic Attack DMG Bonus of all team members by 25% for 15 seconds.

Guide Details

Chixia is a high-firepower Fusion DPS who relies heavily on her continuous Resonance Skill attacks. Focus on utilizing her rapid-fire state and quickly swap to off-field supports who can boost her Fusion damage.
