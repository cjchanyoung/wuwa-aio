Fusion Sword Main / Sub DPS

Changli

Counselor to the Jinzhou Magistrate and mentor to Jinhsi. Possessing a clever mind and a graceful demeanor, Changli commands the battlefield as a powerful Fusion Carry, capable of acting as both a primary Main DPS or a high-multiplier Sub-DPS that buffs incoming Fusion Resonators.

(5★ Resonator)

Weapon Recommendations

Blazing Brilliance 5★ Signature

Base ATK: 587 • Crit DMG: 56.2% (Lvl 90)

Provides massive Crit DMG. Increases ATK by 12%. When dealing damage, grants stacks that increase Resonance Skill DMG by up to 56%. Specially designed to unleash Changli's skill-intensive combat rotation.

Commando of Conviction 4★ Best Alternative

Base ATK: 412 • ATK%: 30.3% (Lvl 90)

When Intro Skill is triggered, increases ATK by 15% for 15s. Excellent F2P weapon with a straightforward trigger condition for quickswap builds.
