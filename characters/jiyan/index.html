Resonance Chain (Sequences)

S1: Benevolence Recommended

Resonance Skill Windqueller can be cast 1 more time. The active time of the skill is increased by 1 second.

S2: Versatility

When casting Intro Skill Tactical Strike, Jiyan recovers 30 Resolve and increases ATK by 28% for 15 seconds.

S3: Spectator

When casting Resonance Liberation Emerald Storm or Intro Skill Tactical Strike, increases Crit Rate by 16% and Crit DMG by 32% for 8 seconds.

S4: Prudence

When casting Resonance Liberation, increases all team members' Heavy Attack DMG by 25% for 30 seconds.

S5: Resoluteness

Outro Skill damage multiplier is increased by 120%. Jiyan's attacks ignore 15% of the target's DEF.

S6: Fortitude Recommended

Increases the damage multiplier of Jiyan's attacks in Qingloong state by an additional 120%.

Gameplay Guidelines & Tips

Jiyan is a highly consistent Aero Main DPS. Pair him with Mortefi to gain a massive Heavy Attack DMG boost from Mortefi's Outro Skill, and make sure to swap to Jiyan when his concerto energy is full to activate his high-impact entry attacks.
