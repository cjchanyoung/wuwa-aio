Gameplay Guidelines & Tips

Jinhsi is a highly explosive burst DPS whose main damage comes from her Forte Circuit. Coordinate with sub-DPS units (like Yinlin or Yuanwu) who can deal coordinated attacks to quickly build Jinhsi's Forte Circuit stacks.
