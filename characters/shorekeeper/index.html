Shorekeeper

The mysterious and divine guardian of the Black Shores, tethered directly to the vital systems of the island. Shorekeeper represents a top-tier support unit, providing team-wide healing, Crit Rate/Crit DMG scaling buffers, and crucial utility through her starlight and butterfly manifestations.

Gameplay Guidelines & Tips

Shorekeeper is a premier healer. Her main goal is to activate her Resonance Liberation domain, which upgrades through three levels as team members enter and exit the zone. Ensure she reaches 250% ER for continuous domain uptime.
