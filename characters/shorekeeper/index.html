Shorekeeper

The mysterious and divine guardian of the Black Shores, tethered directly to the vital systems of the island. Shorekeeper represents a top-tier support unit, providing team-wide healing, Crit Rate/Crit DMG scaling buffers, and crucial utility through her starlight and butterfly manifestations.

(5★ Resonator)

Weapon Recommendations

Stellar Symphony 5★ Signature

Base ATK: 412 • HP%: 77.0% (Lvl 90)

Shorekeeper's signature weapon. Provides massive HP% scaling and substantial Energy Regen. When casting Resonance Liberation, triggers team-wide ATK% and Crit Rate enhancements.

Variation 4★ Best Alternative

Base ATK: 337 • Energy Regen: 51.8% (Lvl 90)

Grants exceptionally high Energy Regen. Restores 8 Concerto Energy upon casting Resonance Skill, vastly speeding up team rotation speeds. Excellent F2P utility.
