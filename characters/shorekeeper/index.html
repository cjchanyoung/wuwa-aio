Resonance Chain (Sequences)

S1: Unspoken Conjecture Recommended

Increases the radius of Stellarealms by 150% and their duration by 10 seconds. Intro Skill no longer ends Stellarealm prematurely.

S2: Night's Gift and Refusal Recommended

The Outer Stellarealm now grants a 40% ATK Bonus to team members inside it.

S3: Infinity Awaits Me

Casting Resonance Liberation grants an additional 20 Concerto Energy (once every 25 seconds).

S4: Overflowing Quietude

Increases the Healing Bonus of her Resonance Skill by 70%.

S5: Echoes in Silence

Increases the pulling effect range of her Basic Attack 3 by 50% and her Forte Heavy Attack (Illation) by 30%.

S6: To The New World

Multiplicatively increases base damage multiplier of Intro Skill by 42% and increases Crit DMG by 500%.

Gameplay Guidelines & Tips

Shorekeeper is a premier support unit providing team-wide healing and massive Crit Rate/Crit DMG scaling buffs. Always cast her Resonance Liberation to establish the Stellarealm domain and keep her Energy Regen above 250%.
