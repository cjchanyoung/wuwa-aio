About Us & Architecture

Zero-cost serverless synchronized spreadsheet framework behind WuWa.AIO.

Our Core Mission

WuWa.AIO was created by active players to solve a major issue in modern gaming resources: slow updates and high maintenance costs. Most guide websites suffer from outdated recommendations because updating code is tedious. By pulling data directly from shared community spreadsheets, our site updates instantly whenever players input new build findings.

Serverless Synchronization Architecture

Phase 1

Crowdsourcing

Resonator builders and theorycrafters register custom builds and rotators into our shared Google Sheets queue.

Phase 2

Sheet CSV Export

Google Sheets catalogs inputs and publishes them as a fast, cached public web CSV endpoint, acting as a free database.

Phase 3

Frontend Hydration

Our lightweight Javascript client parses the CSV strings, matches language translations, and renders pages in under 50ms.
